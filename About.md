
# IAM-Driven Compliance Framework for SOC 2 & ISO 27001

In this project, I developed an Identity and Access Management (IAM) framework tailored to meet Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) requirements for SOC 2 and ISO 27001. I focused on secure access controls, audit readiness, and seamless integration with existing compliance processes.

My framework ensures:
- Automated user provisioning and de-provisioning  
- Detailed audit trails and reporting  
- Alignment of IAM policies with compliance controls  

## 📌 Project Overview

My goal was to demonstrate how robust IAM practices can underpin compliance efforts. By designing role-based access reviews, policy management, and risk assessments, I built a solution that streamlines compliance operations and enhances security posture.

## 🔍 Real-World Applicability

### 🎯 Problem It Solves
> “How can organizations automate IAM processes to satisfy auditor requirements for SOC 2 and ISO 27001?”

In practice:
- Manual access reviews are time-consuming and error-prone  
- Auditors need clear evidence of policy adherence  
- Risk assessments and incident logging must be auditable  

### ✅ Where It’s Used
- Enterprises adhering to SOC 2 and ISO 27001 standards  
- Companies needing automated compliance reporting  
- Organizations seeking to strengthen their security governance
